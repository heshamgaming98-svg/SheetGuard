HOW IT WORKS

Deterministic first. AI optional later.

SheetGuard calculates its score from actual workbook findings. It does not use a fixed demo score. Formula errors, suspicious hard-coded cells, duplicate rows, blank-heavy columns, and formula pattern breaks are derived from the uploaded workbook.
